01 Brand Positioning
Who We Are
CARO
A Sri Lankan streetwear brand built for the generation that refuses to be defined. We make graphic tees that hit different — bold, oversized, unapologetic.
Our Mission
To give Sri Lanka's Gen Z a uniform they actually want to wear. No compromise between quality and identity — you get both, or you get nothing.
Target Audience
Men and women aged 16–30. Culturally fluid, digitally native, style-conscious. They know what's real and they don't wear brands that don't mean something.
Our Unique Position
Sri Lankan-made streetwear with a global aesthetic. We're not chasing trends — we're building the culture from the inside out. Local roots. Global vision.

06 Product Tier System
Tier 01 — Drop Collection
DROP
Limited units. Bold, statement graphics. Full countdown ritual. Never restocked. Priced Rs. 3,000–4,500. This is the cultural engine — everything people talk about, post about, and compete for.
Tier 02 — Core Essentials
CORE
Always available. Minimal wordmark and tonal designs. No hype cycle. Replenishable inventory. Priced Rs. 2,500–3,000. This is the entry point — the piece you wear every day while you wait for the next drop.
Design Rules by Tier
DROP COLLECTION
— Bold graphic statements, full-face prints
— Seasonal or concept-driven imagery
— Artist collaborations allowed
— Volt accent colour permitted as feature
— Every piece must feel like an event
CORE ESSENTIALS
— Wordmark only, or minimal tonal print
— No large graphic statements
— Black, bone, and charcoal colourways only
— If it could be a drop, it's too good for Core
— Consistency over novelty — same SKUs, season to season
07 Logo Usage
Primary — on dark
Reversed — on light
Accent — on Volt
Never — low contrast
Clear Space
Always maintain a minimum clear space equal to the height of the "C" in the logo around all sides.
Minimum Size
Digital: 80px wide minimum.
Print: 25mm wide minimum. Below this, use the lettermark only.
Never Do This
Do not stretch, rotate, recolor, add effects, or place on busy backgrounds. The logo is always solid — no gradients.

09 Photography Direction
What to shoot
— Neutral grey/white studio backgrounds (as seen in your shoots)
— Full body and three-quarter poses
— Real models, real expressions — not overly posed
— Both front and back of garments
— Natural, confident, grounded energy
— Multiple model types — age 16–28, diverse looks
Editing style
— Clean, slightly cool-neutral grade
— High contrast, no overexposure
— Keep fabric texture visible
— Consistent shadow treatment across shoots
— No heavy filters or oversaturated edits
— Avoid warm, golden, "influencer" tones
